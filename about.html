Our Story

Two delivery drivers. One garage. A cloth that doesn't scratch.

Two ex-Amazon drivers, a shared obsession with cars, and a list of complaints about every cloth on the market long enough that we built our own.

April 2026

April 2026

Same delivery round, same conversation

We were driving for Amazon, doing ten-hour shifts back to back. Every route turned into the same chat: what car we'd own one day, and why nothing on the shelf was good enough to look after it properly.

No plan yet

“Just two blokes in hi-vis talking about cars we didn't own.”

May 2026

May 2026

Shifts by day, the idea by night

No big leap. Weeks of doing the routes by day and working out how to actually build the thing in the evenings. Nothing flashy, just trying to turn the conversation from the van into something real.

Late nights

“Staying up for something neither of us could explain properly yet.”

June 2026

June 2026

Got the cars. Then found the problem.

The cars finally showed up, and so did the disappointment. Every cloth we tried failed the same way. We were the customers complaining about the product, which is usually a bad sign for an industry and a decent sign you should build your own.

Every cloth we bought
  • Scratched the paint
  • Needed a dozen passes to dry anything
  • Fell apart after a few washes
  • Cost more than it was worth

July 2026

July 2026

So we built it ourselves

A few frantic weeks, more failed batches than we'll admit to, and 300+ wash tests later, wype® existed. Nothing revolutionary. A cloth built by two people who actually cared whether it left a mark on the bonnet.

The 1-litre test

One litre of water on the deck lid. One towel. One pass.

1200 GSM · 70/30 blend · 300+ wash tests

Now

Out of the garage, onto other people's cars

Meets, shows, Silverstone, Mayfair. Watching someone who's spent six figures on a car trust our cloth with the paint is still the best part of this.
