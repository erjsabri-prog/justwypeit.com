Our first ever event: Porschenomics at Goodwood

On Saturday we packed the car full of towels and headed to the Porschenomics event at Goodwood Motor Circuit. It was wype's first ever event, and honestly, we could not have picked a better one.

Sunshine, an incredible line-up of Porsches on the lawn, and a paddock full of people who care about their cars as much as we do. We handed out towels, talked microfibre all day, and met so many of you who already use wype. Thank you to everyone who stopped by the boot of our 911 for a chat.
