MultiWype+™ is a 36-pack of 40×40 cm general-purpose microfibre cloths, built with a dual-weave pile — a short-pile side for everyday wiping and scrubbing, and a long-pile side for dusting, glass and light polishing. Edgeless construction (heat-sealed, no stitched border) keeps every side safe on glass, screens and gloss plastics.

  • Pack size: 36 cloths
  • Cloth size: 40 × 40 cm
  • Density: 400 GSM
  • Composition: 70/30 microfibre blend — 70% polyester, 30% polyamide (nylon)
  • Construction: Dual-pile weave, edgeless (heat-sealed edges)
  • Care: Machine wash 30°C, no fabric softener, no tumble dry above 40°C
  • Durability: Washable hundreds of times with normal care

The short-pile side has more direct fibre contact, so it's best for everyday wiping, scrubbing and general cleanup. The long-pile side is softer and more cushioned, so it's better suited to dusting, glass, mirrors, screens and light polishing where you want less pressure on the surface.

MultiWype+™ (400 GSM) is a durable general-purpose cloth pack — highly absorbent for everyday spills and cleaning, but not intended to replace a dedicated drying towel.
